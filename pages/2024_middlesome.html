NARRATIVE DESIGN • 2D PUZZLE ADVENTURE

Meddlesome Company

A side-scrolling puzzle adventure exploring miscommunication and corporate espionage through multi-perspective gameplay.

Play the Game ↗
Course IAT 313: Narrative & New Media
Collaborators Jillian Li, Michelle Lin, Keith Law
Platform 2D Side-Scroller
Concept World-Building

The Concept

In Meddlesome Company, players infiltrate the technological giant Melon Corp. The narrative hook relies on an information gap: two high-achieving employees from a rival firm are sent on the same mission, both believing they are working solo.

My role focused on translating this original script into a playable User Experience, ensuring the environmental puzzles complemented the theme of secrecy and accidental collaboration.

Narrative Architecture

The game is structured into three distinct chapters. Players experience the mission first through Anthony’s perspective, then Theo’s, before the perspectives converge in a high-stakes finale. This structure required careful Level Design to ensure that clues discovered in one chapter felt rewarding when resolved in another.

Character: Anthony

Focused on stealth and technical infiltration. His path highlights the high-pressure environment of Berry Inc.

Character: Theo

Focused on environmental navigation and puzzle-solving. His journey reveals the inner workings of Melon Corp.

Visual World-Building

As a 2D adventure, the aesthetic needed to balance the sterile corporate vibe of a tech giant with the playful tension of a puzzle game. I worked on ensuring the Visual Assets maintained a cohesive style that signaled different "security tiers" within the corporate office.

Interactive Mechanics

The puzzles were designed to be "meddlesome"—requiring players to think about how their solo actions might be affecting the unseen teammate. This was a direct application of my **Communications (CMNS)** background, exploring how "non-verbal storytelling" works in a digital space.
