Why a design system?

Every element on this site was designed intentionally — not assembled from a template. This page documents the full visual language: tokens, components, and the reasoning behind each decision. It reflects the same discipline I bring to client work — consistency, accessibility, and a clear hierarchy of purpose.

All components are rendered live from style.css — the single source of truth for the portfolio.

Design Principles

The reasoning that shaped every token and pattern choice.

Warmth over neutrality

Ember orange grounds the palette in warmth without aggression. It signals energy and craft — not corporate polish.

Hierarchy through weight

One typeface, four weights. Contrast comes from size and weight — not colour — keeping the system legible and accessible.

Motion with purpose

Every animation has a semantic reason: breathe signals life, card lift signals clickability, scroll bounce signals more content below.

Consistent roundness

From 6px nav links to 32px cards, the radius scale is deliberate — softer on larger surfaces, tighter on small interactive elements.

Single-source CSS

All 12+ pages share one style.css. Changing a token changes the whole portfolio — no duplication, no drift.

Against Corporate Memphis

Assets are grounded in narrative meaning — growth, motion, environment — not decorative cheerfulness. Every illustration reinforces content.
